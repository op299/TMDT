## Brand & Style
This design system expresses an **Artisan Heritage & Provenance Trust** identity, rooted in the traditional backstrap loom brocade of the Cơ Tu community in Đông Giang, Quảng Nam. The visual ethos merges centuries-old indigenous weaving geometry with contemporary editorial luxury. 

The aesthetic is grounded, organic, respectful, and authoritative:
- **Heritage & Provenance:** Visualizing authenticity and tracing the lineage of each weave through tactile warmth, organic hues, and dedicated provenance markers (OCOP verification, artisan identification, and geographic tracing).
- **Cultural Safety Gate:** Traditional geometric motifs, interlaced bands, and diamond lozenge patterns are respected as sacred cultural markers rather than mere background noise. Motifs must never be distorted, inverted, cropped disrespectfully, or layered behind illegible content.
- **Atmosphere:** Natural, hand-woven, enduring. Evoking the scent of natural plant dyes, mountain cotton, forest canopies, and baked clay earth.

## Colors
The color palette derives directly from natural indigenous dye ingredients: mountain forest foliage (Forest Green `#1B5E42`), baked clay & madder root (Terracotta Coral `#C84B2F`), raw unbleached cotton fibers (Warm Cream `#F5EDE0`), riverbed silt (Surface Sand `#EDE0CC`), and deep iron-rich mud dyes (Dark Canopy `#1A2E22`).

### Contrast & Cultural Roles
- **Warm Cream (`#F5EDE0`)**: Serves as the primary canvas, replacing harsh digital white `#FFFFFF` with warm woven naturalness.
- **Surface Sand (`#EDE0CC`)**: Provides gentle container elevation and card differentiation without requiring artificial drop shadows.
- **Forest Green (`#1B5E42`)**: The primary brand driver, symbolizing the Truong Son mountain home of the Cơ Tu people. Used for high-priority actions, verified OCOP badges, and active interactive elements.
- **Terracotta Coral (`#C84B2F`)**: Secondary high-energy accent for purchase paths, artisan callouts, and loom highlight markers.
- **Dark Canopy (`#1A2E22`) & Earth Brown (`#3D3028`)**: Deliver exceptional readability (WCAG AAA contrast on Cream `#F5EDE0` and Sand `#EDE0CC`), completely eliminating synthetic pure black.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy establishes a three-tier narrative rhythm:
1. **Playfair Display (Display & Headings):** Conveys editorial reverence, legacy, and craft heritage. It commands the chapter titles, product series names, and key cultural headers.
2. **Lora (Italicized Storytelling & Quotes):** Captures the voice of Cơ Tu artisans, quotes from village elders, poetic storytelling layers, and provenance narratives. Rendered in italic by default to create a handwritten, personal connection.
3. **Inter (Interface, Data, & Body):** High-clarity utilitarian sans-serif ensuring microcopy, sizing tables, material composition, pricing, and provenance certification data remain crisp and universally accessible across all screen sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column grid system for desktop (breakpoints: `1200px+`), scaling down to 8 columns on tablet (`768px - 1199px`), and 4 columns on mobile (`<768px`).

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are punctuated by structural intervals of `space-2xl` (64px) on desktop and `space-xl` (40px) on mobile to mimic the deliberate pauses of editorial broadsheets.
- **Weave Border Insets:** Cultural woven ribbon dividers (`h-8` to `h-12`) act as structural horizon lines between narrative transitions (e.g., from the Story 5-layers into the Products Catalog, and preceding the Provenance/OCOP chamber).

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects synthetic drop shadows and diffuse blurred ambient lighting. Instead, depth is articulated through **Textile Tonal Layering** and **Tactile Tactility**:

1. **Layered Surfaces:** The base canvas is `#F5EDE0` (Warm Cream). Elevated modules, product display tiles, and narrative drawers sit on `#EDE0CC` (Surface Sand).
2. **Weft & Warp Borders:** Structural boundaries utilize 1px solid borders in `#1B5E42` at 15% opacity or `#1A2E22` at 12% opacity, simulating woven seams rather than hard vector strokes.
3. **Cultural Ribbon Trim:** Visual anchors between top-tier sections feature the authentic Cơ Tu interlaced border (terracotta and green bands interwoven with diamond lozenge beads).
4. **Focused Modals & Popovers:** Where physical elevation is mandatory (e.g., QR provenance modal scan), a crisp layered outline of `2px solid #1A2E22` accompanied by a flat offset shadow (`4px 4px 0px #1A2E22`) is employed to reinforce hand-stamped craft certification.

## Shapes
A subtle **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** standard is applied across interactive containers:
- Base elements (buttons, badge tags, input fields) use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Larger containers (cards, story sheets, gift set modules) use `0.5rem` (8px).

This maintains the crisp, structured discipline of woven textile grids (which inherently follow orthogonal warp-and-weft angles) while softening corners just enough to feel warm and inviting to touch. Full pill shapes are strictly reserved for provenance verification seals and circular artisan portrait framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Loom Green):** Background `#1B5E42`, text `#F5EDE0`, border-radius `4px`, padding `12px 24px`. On hover: `#144631`. Active: slight inset scale (98%).
- **Secondary Action (Terracotta):** Background `#C84B2F`, text `#F5EDE0`, border-radius `4px`, padding `12px 24px`. Reserved for checkout paths and limited artisan releases.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Border `1.5px solid #1B5E42`, background transparent, text `#1B5E42`. On hover: fills with `#EDE0CC`.

### Trust Strip & OCOP Badges
- Displayed horizontally in a 5-unit distribution across desktop, scrolling smoothly on mobile.
- Enclosed in subtle `#EDE0CC` frames with a 1px `#1B5E42` (20% opacity) border.
- Features micro-engraved icons paired with `label-caps` in `#1A2E22`.

### Cards (Product & Story Tiles)
- Built on `#EDE0CC` surfaces with `rounded-lg` (8px) corners.
- Images display product photography against authentic warm backgrounds, framed with a bottom woven trim pattern.
- Price displayed in `Inter` semi-bold `#1A2E22`, flanked by an OCOP stars badge or Đông Giang origin stamp.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Background `#F5EDE0`, border `1px solid #1A2E22` (30% opacity). Focus state: border `2px solid #1B5E42` with zero fuzzy glow. Text color `#3D3028`.
- Checkboxes & Radios: Square or circular form with custom checkmarks in `#1B5E42`, avoiding system blues or purples entirely.

### Provenance Chamber (OCOP + QR Verification)
- A specialized card module with a certificate motif: bordered by a dual-tone interwoven Cơ Tu motif. Includes high-contrast dynamic QR container, digital certification code, and artisan signature seal.
